Volunteer & community work

Community Services Access

Helping people overcome barriers and connect with free or low-cost community services through practical, respectful support.

Purpose

Make community services easier to find and access.

Listen

Understand each person’s goals, preferences, questions, and barriers without making assumptions.

Connect

Help identify relevant community services, clarify eligibility information, prepare for next steps, and make informed referrals when appropriate.

Respect

Protect dignity, privacy, choice, and each person’s right to decide which support is useful.

Stories, photos & privacy

Photos and stories will be published only with clear, informed permission from the person shown and any required parent, guardian, or organization approval. Captions will avoid sensitive details; images may be omitted, cropped, or de-identified whenever privacy or dignity could be affected.

Statistics will be aggregated and reviewed to avoid identifying individuals or very small groups. No names, contact information, case details, or confidential service records will be published.
