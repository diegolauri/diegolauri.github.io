Professional portfolio area

IT Support

User-centered technical support built on active listening, methodical troubleshooting, and clear follow-through.

Service approach

Understand the issue. Restore service. Leave useful notes.

Listen & clarify

Gather symptoms, context, impact, and prior steps in language that respects the user’s time and experience.

Troubleshoot

Check the simplest likely causes first, test carefully, verify resolution, and avoid disrupting data or workflows.

Document & escalate

Record the issue, steps taken, outcome, and next owner so that support remains consistent and auditable.

Support areas

End-user support

Account access, common software questions, device setup, and practical user guidance.

Systems & connectivity

Foundational troubleshooting for operating systems, peripherals, networks, and productivity tools.

Knowledge management

Clear tickets, repeatable checklists, and help documentation that makes future requests easier to resolve.

Professional standards

Support work should protect user privacy and system integrity. Portfolio examples will be anonymized and will not include credentials, personal data, private screenshots, or confidential organizational information.
